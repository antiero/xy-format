xybuddy privacy policy

xybuddy doesn’t record, upload or collect any song, project or backup data from your OP–XY.

This policy covers the xybuddy website at xybuddy.xyz and the xybuddy apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac. xybuddy is made by antiero (5of12). It isn’t affiliated with teenage engineering.

your files stay on your device

Imports, conversions and exports run on your device or in your browser. MIDI files, OP–XY projects and backups are never sent to xybuddy.

There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The website doesn’t set cookies and doesn’t load third-party scripts or fonts.

the step counter

When you convert a project or MIDI file, the website sends one number to api.xybuddy.xyz: how many steps were converted. That number is added to the running total shown on the site.

Your files are never sent. No file names, contents or project data go with the count. The server keeps only two totals: steps converted and number of conversions.

backups and iCloud

The apps save backups, generated projects and captured audio on your device, or in a folder you choose. If you choose iCloud for backups, they’re stored in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account, and handled by Apple. The developer can’t see them.

what the apps connect to

  • teenage.engineering: to check for new OP–XY firmware, the apps read teenage engineering’s public downloads page. If you update, the firmware file downloads from there too. Nothing about you or your OP–XY is sent.
  • xybuddy.xyz: the Song editor is built into the apps. If it can’t load from the app, it loads from xybuddy.xyz, and the step counter above applies.
  • Links to guides and forums open in your browser, under those sites’ own policies.

hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the step counter runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may log standard request details, such as IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies.

questions

For any privacy questions, get in contact with 5of12 at info@5of12.co.uk. If you email us, we only use your address to reply.

Song editor bug reports go to GitHub issues, which are public. Anything you post there is covered by GitHub’s own privacy policy.

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
